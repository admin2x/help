## Still need help?

Admin2x gets a high volume of requests for personal cybersecurity help, so all of it runs through our support ticket system rather than through phone calls, texts, or screen-sharing sessions. Before opening a ticket, please read the article above and complete the steps it describes; most tickets are resolved by the first step alone. If you have done that and something specific is still not working, open a ticket and tell us exactly which step failed and what you saw on the screen.

To open a support request ticket, send an email to `support` `@` `admin2x.com` or use the [Admin2x Support Portal](https://admin2x.zohodesk.com/portal/en/home){target=_blank}, where you can open a support request ticket or check the status of a ticket you already opened. Tickets are answered in the order received.
